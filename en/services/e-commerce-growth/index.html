Frequently asked questions

Which e-commerce platforms do you work with?

We work with website platforms that support the Meta Pixel and catalog connection. We discuss the setup for your platform in the consultation.

What is ROAS?

ROAS shows how much revenue each lira spent on ads brings in. For example, if you spend 1,000 TL and make 4,000 TL in sales, your ROAS is 4.
