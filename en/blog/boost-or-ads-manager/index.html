“Boost” or Ads Manager? The difference and the right choice

Short answer: Boost turns an existing post into an ad in a few taps — fast but limited. Meta Ads Manager gives full control over the campaign objective, audience, testing and conversion tracking. Boost can be enough to quickly spread one post; if you want steady messages, sign-ups or sales, a well-built Ads Manager setup is always more efficient.

“We got messages when we boosted posts, but now that we run ads nobody writes.” We hear this from business owners all the time. The issue isn't the button itself, it's the ad setup behind it.

What is Boost?

A quick tool that turns an existing post into an ad in a few taps. It's easy, but its targeting, placement and measurement options are limited.

What does Ads Manager do differently?

  • The right objective: optimize for a clear result like messages, leads or sales.
  • Audience control: use location, interests, lookalikes and retargeting.
  • Testing: try different images and copy at once and find the winner.
  • Measurement: with the pixel and conversion tracking you see what the ad really brings in.

Why do results drop after switching to Ads Manager?

Ads Manager is powerful, but set up wrong it can perform worse than Boost. A wrong objective, an oversized audience or budgets split too thin are the usual causes. We cover them in 5 reasons your ad gets no messages.

Which one should you choose?

To quickly show one post to more people, Boost may be enough. If you want steady messages, sign-ups or sales, a well-built Ads Manager setup is always more efficient in the long run. Want to set it up yourself? In our live training we build it together on your own account.

Frequently asked questions

Is boosting a post the same as running an ad?

No. Boost is an ad too, but its targeting, testing and measurement options are far more limited than Ads Manager.

Should a small business use Ads Manager?

Yes, if it wants steady customers. Even on a small budget, the right objective and a focused audience make Ads Manager more efficient.
