4 rules for reaching the right parents with preschool ads

Short answer: The 4 rules for preschool ads that bring enrollments: choose a narrow radius around the school, build trust with real classroom and teacher content, answer “what's your price?” with your difference and a visit invitation rather than just a number, and spread the budget before and during enrollment season.

In preschool advertising, high views don't always mean high enrollment. When choosing a school for their child, parents don't just look at activities or price; first they look for trust. In our Meta ads management for preschools, these are the four rules we focus on most.

1. Why narrow the location?

Parents mostly prefer schools close to home or work. Pick a sensible radius around your school; impressions on the other side of the city waste your budget. It's one of the most common reasons ads get no messages.

2. How do you put trust first?

Real classroom photos, teacher introductions, safety measures and parent reviews bring far more messages than stock images. Parents want to see inside the school.

3. How should you answer “What's your price?”

If a parent who asked about price never writes back, the price isn't always too high. In your reply, give your school's difference, an invitation to visit and a clear next step. Just sending a number ends the conversation.

4. When should you spend the budget?

Build awareness before enrollment season, then focus on message and lead campaigns during it. Leaving the whole budget for the last week is expensive and leaves no time to build trust. This requires Ads Manager rather than Boost.

The result

When the right location, trust-building content and a well-managed message flow come together, your budget starts bringing enrollments instead of just views. You can request a free consultation for your school.

Frequently asked questions

What location radius should a preschool ad use?

It depends on the neighborhood and transport, but generally a few kilometers that parents can reach daily is enough.

What content brings the most messages in preschool ads?

Real classroom photos, teacher introductions, safety measures and parent reviews build more trust and bring more messages than stock images.
